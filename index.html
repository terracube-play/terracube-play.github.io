FEATURES

A survival sandbox that runs in a single browser tab and installs like an app.

❤️Survival

Hearts, hunger, food, armor, drowning, lava and fall damage. Sleep in a bed to skip the night.

🔨Crafting and furnace

A 2×2 grid in your inventory and a 3×3 grid at the crafting table, with shaped recipes. Smelt ore and cook food.

⛏️Tools, armor and tiers

Wood to diamond pickaxes, axes, shovels, swords and armor. Obsidian needs a diamond pickaxe.

🏘️Villages and trading

Find villages with houses, wells and chests. Trade with farmers, toolsmiths and masons for emeralds, tools and armor.

🔥The Nether

Build an obsidian frame, light it with flint and steel and step through. Lava seas, glowstone caves, fortresses and blazes.

🐉The End

Craft eyes of ender, find the stronghold, open the End portal and fight the Ender Dragon among the obsidian pillars.

💾Worlds that save

All three dimensions autosave in your browser. Start a new world any time and tune the settings.

📱Install and play anywhere

Works as an installed app with touch controls on phones, and keyboard and mouse on desktop.

BLOCKS AND ITEMS

Seventy-five blocks, items, tools and armor pieces to find, craft and build with.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Terracube is free and runs entirely in your browser.

Will my world be saved?

Yes. It autosaves in this browser every 20 seconds and when you pause or leave. Clearing the site's data deletes it.

Can I install it?

Yes. Use your browser's Install option, or Add to Home Screen on a phone. Once installed it opens like an app.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. On touch screens you get an on-screen stick, a look area and buttons for mine, use, jump and inventory.

How do I get to the Nether?

Mine obsidian with a diamond pickaxe (it hides deep underground), build a frame at least 4 wide and 5 tall, then right-click the inside with flint and steel. Craft flint and steel from an iron ingot and coal.

How do I reach the End?

Kill blazes in a Nether fortress for blaze rods and endermen for pearls. Craft eyes of ender, right-click one to point toward the stronghold, dig down, and place eyes in the portal frames.

Is there multiplayer?

Not yet. Terracube is a single-player game for now.

The game is slow. What can I do?

Lower the render distance in the pause menu, and use a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox with hardware acceleration on.
